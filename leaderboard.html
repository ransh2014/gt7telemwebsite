Can't find your track?

GT7's telemetry never reports a track ID, so new circuits only get added when someone tells us the name. Type it below — it goes straight into the review queue and gets added to this picker (and the app's own TRACK dropdown) once confirmed.

Can't find your car?

Missing a car from the list above? Type its name below, plus its numeric Car ID if you have it — that's the fastest way for it to get added correctly.
Where to find the Car ID: open TRACE's Live Dashboard while that car is on track — the SESSION / MISC panel has a "CAR ID" box showing the number. No dashboard handy? Leave it blank and just submit the name.
