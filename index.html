What's Inside

Four Tools.
One Package.

01
Live Dashboard

Real-time telemetry from GT7 over UDP — speed, RPM, gear, tyre temps, fuel, G-forces, and 40+ more channels. All live, all in one window. Cars and tracks are shown by name via ddm999's gt7info database, not a raw ID.

02
Track Map

Live mini map drawn from world coordinates. Your position traces the circuit in real time as you drive.

03
Lap Analyst

19 chart groups post-session — inputs, tyres, engine, braking, G-force, sector timing, heat maps, driver ratings, and A vs B lap comparison. Submit to a global per-car, per-track leaderboard, download any Top-10 lap as a ghost, or overlay a community-average racing line. See Privacy for what's submitted.

04
Race Analyst

Full-race replay and comparison - load two complete race sessions side by side, replay them synced, score every lap corner by corner, check how consistent you were, and export the breakdown to CSV, HTML or a shareable race card.

Free · Open Source · Windows / macOS / Linux / Android
